Short-Form Submission

Write a 600–1,500 word short-form article, organized into Roman-numeral sections with Bluebook citations, and submit it through our article submission form. We publish every two weeks.

Next deadline: Friday, October 23rd at 11:59 PM (published October 26th)
Volume I short-form deadline: November 20th at 11:59 PM

Preface

You do not have to propose what you are writing to us about, BUT we have the right to refuse to publish anything that does not meet the ULR’s standards, including but not limited to:

  • Any form of plagiarism or AI
  • Any form of harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
  • Poor writing, and/or short or over the word limit

Word Count

Between 600 and 1,500 words

Included in the Article

  • Title
  • Sections divided into parts, numbered by Roman numerals
  • Citations at the end in Bluebook style (include footnotes/superscripts)
  • 1.5 spacing and 1.5-inch margins
  • Please use ENDNOTES
  • USE GOOGLE DOCS!

Submission

We will publish short-form every two weeks; your first round of submissions will be due Oct 9th and published Oct 12th, and so on every two weeks. When you have finished your short-form article, please submit it in the article submission form by the Friday of that two-week period at 11:59pm. If you submit it late, it will not be published until the following publication period.

In the Article Submission Form You Will Need To

  • The link to your article on an editable Google Doc
  • Provide a two-sentence summary that will be posted on our LinkedIn and Instagram
  • Provide a cover image for your article
  • Your handles for us to tag you
