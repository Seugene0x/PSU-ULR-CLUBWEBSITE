Spring 2027 E-Board Applications

Thank you for your interest in joining the Executive Board of the Undergraduate Law Review at Penn State! The Undergraduate Law Review provides students with an opportunity to explore different legal issues, developing their skills in legal research and writing. Our goal is to create a publication every semester that encourages thoughtful discussion of legal topics while helping students strengthen their academic and professional skills.

Treasurer

The Treasurer is responsible for overseeing the organization's finances, maintaining accurate financial records, and helping ensure that the Undergraduate Law Review has the resources necessary to operate successfully.

Secretary

The Secretary will keep records of E-Board meetings, including meeting minutes, important decisions, and upcoming deadlines. They will also help keep the board organized and make sure members stay informed about important updates. The Secretary will also be the main point of contact for members of the Undergraduate Law Review.

Event Planner

The Event Planner will come up with a minimum of 2 events monthly and execute them for all members. They will help organize different activities for the Undergraduate Law Review. This includes developing, organizing, and executing events that support the Undergraduate Law Review's academic, professional, and organizational goals.

Outreach Chair

The Outreach Chair will connect the Undergraduate Law Review with legal professionals and organizations to create opportunities for members. Their responsibilities include building relationships between the Undergraduate Law Review and the Penn State community, including students, organizations, faculty, departments, and external professionals. They should invite them to speak at club meetings, as well as research potential field trips, law-related conferences, and other professional development opportunities for members.

Public Relations Chair

The PR Chair will manage the Undergraduate Law Review’s social media accounts and help promote the organization to the Penn State community. They are responsible for managing the Undergraduate Law Review's public image and social media presence in a way that reflects the organization's academic and legal focus.

Fundraising Chair

The Fundraising Chair will develop a minimum of 2 fundraising ideas monthly and organize initiatives to raise money for the Undergraduate Law Review. They will work with the E-Board to identify fundraising opportunities and help secure funds for publications, events, and other organizational needs.

Expectations

E-Board members are expected to attend weekly meetings, communicate with other members, complete their assigned tasks, and meet deadlines. Members should be willing to contribute outside of meetings and work together to help the Undergraduate Law Review grow. Applicants should be prepared to take their roles seriously while balancing their other academic and extracurricular commitments.
